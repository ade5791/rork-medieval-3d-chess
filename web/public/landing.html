King's Gambit

Cinematic 3D chess in a torch-lit castle hall. Legal play is enforced by a real chess engine; every capture is staged as an animated duel between two sculpted pieces. Runs entirely in the browser - nothing to install.

What is in the build

Four civilisations

Age of Kings, Imperial Rome, Sengoku Japan and New Kingdom Egypt, each with its own sculpted roster.

Four battlegrounds

Sun Temple, Dawn Court, Frostfall and Siege at Dusk - separate skies, light rigs and ground treatments.

A real engine

chess.js rules with a search engine on a Web Worker, so thinking never blocks the frame. Three difficulties.

Animated combat

Captures resolve through an explicit state machine - approach, strike, death - driven by rigged clips, not guesswork.

Play a human

Pass-and-play hotseat on one device. Optional clocks. Undo takes back a ply.

Quality presets

Four graphics tiers with shader pre-warm behind the loading screen, so play does not stall on first compile.

Controls

Every binding below was read out of the shipped source. Keys act only during a live match, and are ignored while typing in a field or when held with Ctrl/Cmd/Alt.

InputAction
Tap / click a piece, then a squareSelect, then move. Legal destinations are highlighted.
Drag on empty spaceOrbit the camera. Wheel or pinch to zoom.
FFlip the camera to the other side of the board.
TToggle the tactical (overhead) shot.
CCinema mode - hide the interface for a clean view.
HOpen or close the chronicle (move history).
EscClose settings, the camera menu, or the chronicle.
SpacePause or resume - in demo mode only.

Camera

Four presets are available from the HUD camera menu: white side, black side, top-down, and cinematic.

Verified before publishing

  • 130 of 130 player-journey checks pass against these exact bytes, across desktop, touch portrait, touch landscape and reduced-motion surfaces.
  • Zero console errors and zero page errors across every surface journey.
  • On-screen state is cross-checked against chess.js FEN, so what you see matches the rules engine.
  • All touch targets measure at least 44px; no overlapping action zones.
  • Rotating the device never resets the game.
  • Every file served live is byte-identical to the build that passed the gate.
Honest limits. Online play against a remote opponent needs a WebSocket relay server, which static hosting cannot run - so on this deployment the online lobby has no relay to reach. Hotseat and play-versus-computer work fully. Touch results come from emulated viewports with synthesized input, not physical handsets. Performance was measured on a desktop RTX 3090; your device will differ.
Built with Vite, React 19, TypeScript and three.js. Board rules by chess.js. Piece models are rendered from generated rigged assets.
