feat(eras): add Sengoku Japan and Egyptian New Kingdom civilisations

Extends the game from 2 to 4 playable historical eras, each with its own
fully rigged, animated 6-kind roster and matching battleground.

New eras
- sengoku (Sengoku Japan, dusk battleground): daimyo, onna-bugeisha,
  sohei, yagura, kiba-musha, ashigaru
- egypt  (Egyptian New Kingdom, dawn battleground): pharaoh, great royal
  wife, hem-netjer, pylon, maryannu charioteer, medjay

Every piece ships rigged with 4 clips (idle / attack / death / walk),
served from public/models/<era>/ instead of expiring vendor URLs.
Also fills the previously-missing rome knight, which had been silently
falling back to the classic medieval sculpt.

Provenance gate
gate-era.mjs gains two checks that the old gate could not express:
- "roster covers every kind" - a missing kind is a hard failure rather
  than a silent fallback
- "no foreign sculpts" - asserts every staged figure's source URL
  belongs to the requested era

This closes a real blind spot: the fallback figure is itself skinned, so
"every figure rigged 29/29" passed while the wrong civilisation was on
the board. Verified by tools/mutate-era-gate.mjs, which deletes the
egypt knight, confirms the gate FAILS on both provenance checks, then
restores and confirms it passes.

Evidence
- 4 eras x 11 checks, 0 failures (tools/out/era-gate2.log)
- 72/72 clips bind at 100% track resolution, min drift 42.49 deg, no
  dead clips (tools/out/bind-report.json)
- beat timeouts 1 vs baseline 1 on every era - no turn-loop regression
- tsc --noEmit clean, production build green
